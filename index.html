Common questions

How big are the batches?

Eight students or fewer, so teachers can check every child's work during class.

What do I need for online classes?

A laptop, tablet or phone with a stable internet connection. Recordings are shared after each class.

Can we change batches later?

Yes. If the pace or timing doesn't suit, your mentor will move your child to a better fit.

Is the demo class really free?

Yes. There is no payment and no card needed.
